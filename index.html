Research release

Torino Urban EPW Atlas

Authors: Ali JahaniRahaei and Giacomo Chiesa.

The atlas links a 100 m assignment grid to morphology measured over overlapping 500 m supports. Seven retained morphology classes are reduced to four candidate UWG weather files.

Modeled UHI values are UWG simulations relative to AvMY Caselle, not measured local UHI. The assigned file reproduces the fixed UWG framework; it is not a guarantee of local observational accuracy. Municipal-edge supports and morphology outliers are explicitly flagged.

For 20 high-density C6 supports, the location recommendation is withheld because the common building-stock assumptions have a demonstrated applicability limit. Dark cells retain their morphology labels. A location below this threshold is not guaranteed accurate.
