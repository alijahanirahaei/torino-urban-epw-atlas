# Authors

The Torino Urban EPW Atlas is authored by:

1. Ali JahaniRahaei
2. Giacomo Chiesa

Both authors should be included when citing the atlas, its derived datasets, UWG
configurations, figures, or generated EPW library. Machine-readable citation
metadata are provided in `CITATION.cff`; the recommended data attribution is in
`DATA_LICENSE.md`.

Repository: https://github.com/alijahanirahaei/torino-urban-epw-atlas

The companion article citation and institutional affiliations will be added to
the citation metadata when they become publicly available.
