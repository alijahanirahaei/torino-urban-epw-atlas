## OpenStreetMap

The application requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap's public tile service and
displays the required attribution. OpenStreetMap data are licensed under the Open
Database License. Public deployments must comply with the current OpenStreetMap
tile usage policy or use an appropriate alternative tile provider. The atlas uses
the prescribed HTTPS tile endpoint for normal interactive viewing, does not
prefetch or bulk-download tiles, and relies on standard browser caching.

Sources: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and
https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/
