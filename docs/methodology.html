Method

Spatial assignment and weather reduction

Torino is sampled with deterministic 100 x 100 m assignment cells. Each cell center receives an overlapping 500 x 500 m morphology support. The assignment cell identifies where a category applies; the larger support supplies the neighborhood statistics passed to UWG.

Five primary variables are robust-scaled and clustered: building fraction, area-weighted building height, facade-to-site ratio, UWG tree-cover fraction, and UWG grass-cover fraction. K-means diagnostics scan k=2-12, followed by CLARA-style k-medoids for k=4, k=7, and k=8. The raw k=7 solution is retained for physical resolution and interpretability.

Annual UWG outputs from the seven medoids are reduced by exhaustive subset selection using maximum hourly dry-bulb RMSE and cluster-share-weighted mean RMSE. The resulting A-D products are weather categories, not a second k=4 morphology solution.

Model scope

The common configuration uses UWG 5.3.4, AvMY Caselle forcing, a 500 m characteristic length, DOE reference zone 4A, and 3.05 m floor height. The four-file result remains a medoid-level candidate pending stratified within-class simulations.
