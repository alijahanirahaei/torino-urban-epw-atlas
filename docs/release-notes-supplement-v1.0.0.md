# Supplementary Material v1.0.0

By Ali JahaniRahaei and Giacomo Chiesa, 17 September 2026.

Supplement to *Morphology-based classification and error-controlled reduction
of urban weather files with Torino as a case study*.

## Downloads

- `supplementary_material.pdf`: the complete readable methods and diagnostic results.
- `supplementary_material.docx`: editable copy with no tracked revisions or comments.
- `torino_uwg_supplement_v1.0.0.zip`: the document, all 13 supplementary CSV tables,
  six figures, exact UWG configurations, heat-sensitivity results and runner,
  file index, licences, provenance and checksums.
- `supplement_checksums.sha256`: hashes of the three downloads above.

Sections S1-S7 cover spatial inputs, support scale, classification, simulation,
non-medoid evaluation, synchronized station comparison, reuse and heat sensitivity.
The heat runner contains 105 configurations: 84 alternatives and 21 baselines.
Source weather must be obtained separately through the documented dataset DOI.

This is a separately versioned supplement to **atlas v0.2.0**. It changes neither
the atlas's weather library and classifications nor the scientific results.
The four candidate EPWs remain subject to the documented model limitations and
industrial-tail restrictions. Simulated temperature fidelity is not observational
accuracy, and the road-ranked heat scenarios are not measured traffic.
